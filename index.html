Transcriptions

An example of a phonemic transcription of a poem by Dickinson meant to reproduce the way the poet would have read and heard the poem herself. 

Not any sunny tone
nɔt 'ɛnɪ 'sənɪ tɔ:n

From any fervent zone
frɔm 'ɛnɪ 'fɛvɛnt zɔ:n

Find entrance there
faɪnd 'ɛntrəns ðe(ə)

Better a grave of Balm
'bɛtə ə gre:v əv bɑ:m

Toward human nature’s home
tod 'hjumən 'ne:tjuz hɒm

And Robins near
ənd 'rɔbɪnz ne(ə)

Than a stupendous Tomb
ðæn ə stu'pɛndəs tu:m

Proclaiming to the gloom
prɔ'clɛ:mɪŋ tə ['to:] ðə glu:m

How dead we are –
hɒu dɛd wi: æ(ə)

Source: Dickinson, Emily. Emily Dickinson’s Poems: As She Preserved Them. Edited by Cristanne Miller. The Belknap Press of Harvard University Press, 2016, p. 676.
